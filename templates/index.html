How We Work

Our 4-Step Quality Process

Customer satisfaction is our beginning and our end. Every single file passes through a rigorous internal review before it ever reaches your inbox.

01

Intake & Technical Analysis

We review your artwork, requested dimensions, placement (caps, jackets, front), and target fabrics.

02

Fabric-Specific Digitizing

Our team applies custom stitch densities, underlays, and patterns (Satin, Tatami, Run) engineered specifically for your garment type.

03

Double-Check Quality Control

Our dedicated QA department inspects the file for registration accuracy, pulling/pushing compensation, and minimal thread breaks.

04

On-Garment Readiness

Your files are delivered in your required format, fully optimized and ready to stitch or print perfectly.

Our Story

Built on Precision & Craft

At RS Digitize, we bridge the gap between creative artwork and flawless physical production. Founded in India — we specialize in commercial-grade embroidery digitizing and crisp vector art conversion, transforming designs into clean, high-performance files optimized for smooth manufacturing. Whether it's intricate left-chest logos, complex jacket backs, or scalable vector graphics, our expert team ensures minimal thread breaks, perfect stitch registration, and sharp, infinitely scalable paths.

2014

Founded in India

Started as a small digitizing studio serving local garment manufacturers in the textile hub of India.

2018

Expanded to Vector & Print

Added screen printing, vinyl cutting, and digital printing services to offer a complete branding solution.

2022

Corporate & Sports Clients

Onboarded corporate uniform programs and regional sports clubs, scaling our capacity and turnaround speed.

2025
